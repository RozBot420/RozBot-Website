Why Copenhagen Matters To Me

For as long as I can remember, I wanted to visit Copenhagen. I learned about Freetown Christiania ages ago as a kid: abandoned barracks taken over by squatters that grew into a city within a city, with its own governance and rules. I always thought that was incredible and wanted to see it for myself.

It used to be known mostly for drugs and dealing, but now it is cleaner and more touristy while still being a real neighborhood where people live. I want to approach it respectfully: ask before taking photos of people or homes, and spend less time being a shutterbug and more time taking in the local vibe.
