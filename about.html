Living My Somedays

This chapter is the heart of my story. I lost three souls I loved deeply in a short time, and that loss changed how I see time, love, and what matters most.

Love Never Leaves Memory In Motion For My Angels

What If I Fall?

Leaving everything I knew was terrifying: my job, my home, my people, and every familiar routine. I stepped into countries where I knew no one, did not know the language, and could not predict if any of it would work out.

Sometimes we have to take the biggest risks to get the biggest rewards.

My Angels

Mama Bear, Mike, and Rascal are the reason I stepped into this life. This journey is for them.

Mama Bear

The woman who stayed in my corner no matter what. Even through divorce and difficult chapters, her friendship never wavered.

Mike

The dad figure who showed me what unconditional love looks like. He gave me the kind of steady care I had not known before.

Rascal Riley

My anchor, my constant, my safe place in every storm. Through homes, jobs, and relationships, it was always me and Rascal against the world.

Always With Me

Mama Bear and Mike were both diagnosed with pancreatic cancer, and after diagnosis we had only days: Mama Bear passed 40 days later, Mike 10 days after that. Within days of Rascal's prognosis, I had to let him go too.

That shock changed everything. They thought they had more time. We all think we have more time. So I chose my most unrealistic someday and made it real: to live and work in Europe and see the world. Every mile I walk now is in honor of them.
